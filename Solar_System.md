## Yanorra System

### Yanorra

The inhabited world of the system, orbiting Ember Mother with its two moons, Serya and Mirelda. Yanorra’s present orbit was altered during The Drift, leaving its calendar, seasons, and religious traditions permanently marked by celestial disorder.

---

### Nemei

**Type:** Rocky terrestrial planet
**Common title:** The Sister Planet
**Orbit:** Slightly farther from Ember Mother than Yanorra
**Orbital period:** ~389 Yanorran days
**Diameter:** ~0.97 Yanorra diameters
**Mass:** ~0.89 Yanorra masses
**Moons:** Two small captured moons

Nemei is Yanorra’s nearest planetary sister, a cold but possibly living world near the outer edge of the habitable zone. Its faint seasonal shifts from green-white to blue-gray have long inspired speculation that it may have oceans, retreating ice, or even some form of vegetation. To many cultures, Nemei represents reflection, longing, fertility, and the life Yanorra might have lived under different heavens.

---

### Hita

**Type:** Gas giant
**Common titles:** The Storm Shield, The Crooked Guardian
**Orbit:** Beyond Nemei, sharply inclined relative to the other planets
**Orbital period:** ~9.8 Yanorran years
**Diameter:** ~11.6 Yanorra diameters
**Mass:** ~360 Yanorra masses
**Moons:** More than 40 known or suspected
**Rings:** Faint, dusty, fed by captured debris

Hita is the system’s great visible shield, a massive storm-banded giant whose strange upright orbit cuts across the paths of the other worlds. Its gravity helps shape debris from the Amar-Ket Belt, holding back many fragments while occasionally throwing others inward as meteor streams. In religion and folklore, Hita is both protector and danger: the guardian who stands sideways against the heavens, but whose shield sometimes slips.

---

### Amar-Ket Belt

**Type:** Asteroid and debris belt
**Common titles:** The Broken Ring, The Grave Road, The Shattered Crown
**Orbit:** Between Hita and Solaq-Mar
**Composition:** Rock, metal, ice-rich fragments, and dark carbon bodies
**Visibility:** Not visible as individual objects to the naked eye, but associated with recurring meteor showers

The Amar-Ket Belt is the great scar of the Yanorran system. Though invisible as a true ring from the ground, its dust trails produce recurring meteor showers that have shaped centuries of omen, prophecy, and fear. Many traditions describe it as the remains of a broken world, a warning that planets, kingdoms, and divine orders can be shattered.

---

### Solaq-Mar

**Type:** Outer gas giant or ice giant
**Common titles:** The Dark Shepherd, The Black Lantern, The Vanishing Planet
**Orbit:** Far beyond the Amar-Ket Belt
**Orbital period:** ~74 Yanorran years
**Diameter:** ~6.5 Yanorra diameters
**Mass:** ~61 Yanorra masses
**Moons:** At least 9 known
**Rings:** Very dark, nearly invisible

Solaq-Mar is the farthest known planet, a dim blue-black giant that vanishes from ordinary sight for decades at a time. Some generations see it clearly; others know it only through records and inherited stories. Its rare returns are associated with grief, hidden gods, forgotten truths, and things once dismissed as myth becoming visible again.
