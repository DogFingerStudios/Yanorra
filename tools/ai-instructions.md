- This project is a wiki for the world of Yanorra, a fictional setting created by the author. The wiki contains information about the geography, history, culture, and lore of Yanorra, as well as details about its various nations, cities, and other locations.

- Yanorra's setting is on a planet that is very Earth like with similar (if not identical) vegetation, animals, and weather patterns. Similar animals exist, namely humans, horses, dogs, cats, birds, fish, etc. 

- Yanorra has no connection to Earth. There is no canon that aligns Yanorra's time period to any specific era of Earth's. There is no canon that says where in the universe Yanorra is located, or if it is even in the same universe as Earth.

- The wiki is written in the POV of an in-world chronicler, or an in-world wikipedia, and is intended to be a resource for players of the game "Saint Aveline" to learn more about the world they are exploring.
